## M0 — Setup & data spike (2026-10-20)
- **Built:** Vite 8 + React 18 + R3F 8 + Tailwind 4 + TS 5.9 scaffold; `npm run data` pipeline (fetch FX/macro/Big Mac/BIS → build → validate) writing 6 JSON snapshots for all 12 places; blank golden-hour canvas with fonts; refresh-data GitHub Action.
- **Expected:** All §5 endpoints to work as written.
- **Actually:** Frankfurter moved to `api.frankfurter.dev/v1` (`.app` 301s). World Bank `PA.NUS.PPPC.RF` is archived (source 57, stale) → derived as `PA.NUS.PRVT.PP ÷ PA.NUS.FCRF` (household-consumption PPP; within ~0–10% of the archived GDP-based ratio, Switzerland archived value looks like a 1.000 placeholder). `FR.INR.RINR` has no France/Türkiye data and stops 2014 (UK), 2017 (Japan) → added BIS policy rates via API and a derived "policy rate − CPI inflation" real rate. Big Mac has no France row → euro-area (EUZ). BRL/INR ECB data starts 2000. No month-over-month FX move exceeds 40% (largest: TRY Dec 2021 +25.9%).

## M1 — Look & feel (2026-10-20)

- **Actually:** A `Builder` that batches static parts by (geometry, color) into instanced meshes keeps Paris at 116 draw calls *including the shadow pass* and ~9.6k triangles, 120 fps on an M-series Mac (still need a 2020 MacBook Air check). Characters are now the main draw-call cost → instance crowds if we add more. Sky gradient first used world-origin directions and looked all-gold from the raised camera; fixed by measuring from the camera. White HUD text over the cream band of the sky needs a real contrast check (added a tight second text-shadow).

## M2 — Globe, navigation, onboarding (2026-10-23)
- **Built:** Title → make traveler (turntable) → home country (12 cards, blocky SVG icons) → amount (chips) → globe fly-in → marker card → camera dive + fade → Paris → "← Globe". Low-poly globe (8,820 flat faces) colored from a 1° Natural Earth land mask baked by `scripts/build-land-mask.ts`; 12 markers (mini Eiffel for Paris) tinted by **inflation-adjusted** strength vs 10-year average (`src/finance/strength.ts`, tested); traveler stands on the home city; HTML "Cities" list as the accessible alternative; profile saved to localStorage (validated on load). 36 tests.
- **Expected:** Nominal "today vs 10-year average" would be enough for the marker tint.
- **Actually:** Nominal is badly misleading for high-inflation currencies — for a USD traveler the lira is "+173% stronger than average" nominally but **−10%** after inflation; the yen is +31% real. Switched to the real exchange rate (rate × home CPI ÷ destination CPI; annual WB inflation interpolated monthly; 2025/2026 USD inflation carried forward from the latest year — flag in the Data panel). Other fixes from testing: card covered its own marker (hover flicker) → card lifted above marker; sky looked all-gold from the orbiting camera → screen-space sky gradient for the globe; creator camera FOV leaked into the globe → globe resets its lens. Globe: 62 draw calls, 12.3k triangles, 120 fps (M-series).

## M3 — City tabs, 12 cities, Data panel (2026-10-26)
- **Built:** Tabs A–D for every home/destination pair. A: rate, conversion, strength meter vs 10-yr average + inflation-adjusted note. B: "feels like" from World Bank consumption PPP at today's rates, 6-item basket (≈ estimates from one documented U.S. reference basket × PPP; Big Mac row uses actual prices), Big Mac over/under-valuation, blocky basket props pop up in the scene. Data Sources panel (sources, coverage, last observation, every estimate method, event sources) and About panel. 67 tests.
- **Expected:** Plug-in formulas; the real-rate indicator from the World Bank would work.
- **Actually:** (1) WB `FR.INR.RINR` is lending-rate based (Brazil ≈ 37%) → used BIS policy rate − CPI for all 12. (2) The reserves rule flagged the UK/Australia as "thin reserves" — wrong for free-floating majors → reserves treated as neutral for USD/EUR/JPY/GBP/CHF/AUD. (3) PPP and the Big Mac can disagree (Paris: prices 18% lower than the U.S., yet the euro looks 11% overvalued by burgers) — a good teaching point for the PDF. (4) Fact-checked the Learn-more examples against our data (lira lost ~60% vs USD Jan 2021–Dec 2022; Türkiye CPI 72% in 2022). Fixes from testing: event caption trapped inside a transformed panel (portal), crisis fog too thick, near-corner blocks hid landmarks, lumpy icosahedron domes → added a low-poly half-sphere. Paris with rain: 102 draw calls, 12.9k triangles, ~0.9 ms/frame on M-series.

## M4 — Central Bank mode + public deploy (2026-11-01)
- **Built:** Public site on GitHub Pages (rebuilds on every push and after each daily data refresh). Central Bank mode: pure model (`src/game/centralBank/`), 4 scenarios (Switzerland 2014–16, Türkiye 2018–19, UK 2022–24, Japan 2022–23) with starting values from our World Bank/BIS data, each quarter's "world" rate from the partner's real BIS path, sourced "What really happened" reveals, seeded RNG, events, forced devaluation, scoring A–F, results charts vs real exchange rate and policy rate. Modes menu; model explained in the Data panel. 89 tests (direction tests, 1,000 random playthroughs, reproducibility, do-nothing plausibility on real data).
- **Expected:** The brief's equations would work after light coefficient tuning.
- **Actually:** With the policy rate held fixed the brief's model is explosive (UK inflation → 50%, Türkiye growth → 15%) and near-zero-rate Switzerland spirals to −5% deflation. Fixed with textbook additions: real rate vs *expected* inflation (anchored by credibility), inflation anchoring toward target, and saturating real-rate-gap effects. Debt *level* replaced by debt *added during your term* (no clean source for 4 countries' debt; Japan's ~250% would otherwise crush the yen in the model). After tuning, passive Türkiye reproduces history closely (lira −28 to −34% vs actual −36%; inflation ~21–25% vs ~25% peak). Office diorama cut (time): the scenario's city sits behind the desk and turns stormy as your score drops.

## M5a — Timeline accuracy, 1999 start, one font (2026-11-01)
- **Built:** Timeline now spans Jan 1999 → today (the full ECB/euro history) with 5 more sourced events (euro launch 1999, Türkiye 2001, rand record low 2001, Brazil 2002, credit crunch 2007) — 22 total; big year label over the scene; log scale for currencies that moved >8× (lira). Replaced Cormorant Garamond + Inter with one system font everywhere, no italics (team found the serif look "too AI").
- **Expected:** Data might drift from the ECB's own monthly figures.
- **Actually:** Cross-checked all 11 currencies × every month since 1999 against the ECB Data Portal's official monthly averages: identical up to rounding (max 0.0005%). Checked every event's month against the data — each shows the expected move (e.g. Lehman: USD +28% vs AUD; SNB floor removal: CHF +12% vs EUR; Dec 2021: USD +48% vs TRY). Why not the 1990s: the euro and ECB reference rates start in 1999; earlier years would need a second source and have no euro.

## M5b — "Invest there" carry-trade calculator (2026-11-01)
- **Built:** A second view in "Through the years": move your money into the destination currency at any month since 1999, earn its central-bank rate (BIS, monthly compounding), convert back today; compared with earning the home rate, split into interest vs currency effect, annualised, with worst drawdown and a plain-English uncovered-interest-parity verdict. Pure, tested function (`src/finance/carry.ts`, 6 tests incl. the UIP-holds case → zero excess return).
- **Expected:** A markets angle (returns and risk) to complement the macro content, since the project is graded by a finance professor.
- **Actually:** Real data gives both outcomes: a U.S. investor putting $1,000 in lira in Jan 2010 earned +1,374% interest but lost 97% on the currency → $439 (vs $1,293 at home, −80% worst drop); the same trade in pesos → $1,945 (UIP failed — the "super peso" carry paid, with a 35% drawdown); yen → $584.

## M5f — Compare: does PPP hold? (2026-11-02)
- **Built:** A Compare mode (Modes menu): one dot per currency, average yearly inflation gap vs home (World Bank CPI) against average yearly depreciation vs home (ECB annual averages), the 45° PPP line, OLS slope and R², period chips (since 1999 / since 2010 / last 5 years), an "include Türkiye" toggle, hover tooltips, a table view. Pure, tested function `src/finance/pppTest.ts` (6 tests).
- **Expected:** Long-run relative PPP to roughly hold.
- **Actually:** vs USD 1999–2024 slope 1.04, R² 0.97, but mostly thanks to Türkiye; without it slope 0.93, R² 0.64; last five years without it slope 0.44, R² 0.05 (yen fell 6.8%/yr despite lower inflation). The textbook "PPP holds in the long run, not the short run" falls straight out of our data. BRL/INR lack full 1999 ECB rates, so they appear only from 2010.

## M5g — Currency switcher, local starting amounts, NYC fix (2026-11-02)
- **Built:** A currency button on the globe (e.g. "¥ JPY ▾") to change home currency without redoing onboarding; money is converted at today's rate. Quick-pick amounts are now about US$100 / $1,000 / $10,000 in each currency, rounded to 1/1.5/2/3/5 × 10ⁿ (¥150,000, ₩1,500,000, ₹100,000), with a live "≈ $X USD" line. Pure, tested `src/finance/presets.ts` (5 tests). Empire State windows now sit on each setback, on all four sides.
- **Expected:** Fixed 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 for every currency looked wrong in yen, won and rupees (¥1,000 ≈ $6).
- **Actually:** Plain 1/2/5 rounding left picks up to 27% off the dollar target (¥200,000 ≈ $1,270); adding 1.5 and 3 keeps them close. A quick-pick amount maps to the same tier in the new currency; a custom amount is converted and rounded to 3 significant figures.
